# 履歴書 (Rirekisho) — Japanese Resume Guide + Template

**When to use:** Traditional Japanese companies, Japanese-language job postings, or when HR explicitly asks. English-first companies (Mercari, Rakuten, Google Japan, Sony, LY Corp) do **NOT** require this — use the English resume instead. When unsure, submit both.

**Key differences from an English CV (researched from JAC International, GaijinBlog, G Talent):**
- Fixed standardized layout (1–2 pages)
- **Photo required** (4.5cm × 3.5cm or 3cm × 4cm, business attire, taken within 6 months)
- **Personal details required**: date of birth, age, gender, nationality, marital status, address
- Chronological, factual, minimal achievements — it's a *record*, not a sales pitch
- Has a fixed section: 志望動機 (Reason for Application)
- Formal keigo (polite Japanese) for written text
- Never write claims you can't back up — Japanese HR cross-checks credentials
- Two pages is normal in Japan (don't compress to one)

## Standard Rirekisho Structure (top to bottom)

| Section | Japanese | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| Date | 年月日 | Today's date |
| Name | 氏名 | Full name (日本語 if you have it, otherwise katakana/romaji) |
| Photo | 写真 | Paste photo top-right |
| Date of birth | 生年月日 | e.g. 2005年4月1日生 |
| Contact | 連絡先 | Phone, address, email |
| Education | 学歴 | **Oldest → newest**, chronological (opposite of English CV). University: 20XX年4月 入学 / 20XX年3月 卒業見込. End with 以上 |
| Work history | 職歴 | Only if you have jobs. Enter (入社) / Leave (退職) dates separately |
| Licenses & qualifications | 免許・資格 | JLPT N5, English certificates, etc. |
| Reason for application | 志望動機 | 3–5 lines, why THIS company + what you offer |
| Hobbies / interests | 趣味・特技 | 1–2 lines (Japanese culture expects this) |
| Preferred conditions | 本人希望欄 | e.g. work location, start date (write 貴社の規定に従います — "follow company rules" if unsure) |

## 志望動機 (Reason for Application) — template

```
私は、大学でコンピュータサイエンスを専攻し、Pythonとデータ構造・アルゴリズムを学んでおります。
貴社の◯◯（事業内容）に強く魅力を感じ、御社の技術力とグローバルな環境で働きたいと考えています。
現在、日本語能力試験N5に合格しており、さらに日本語を磨きながら、御社の◯◯事業に貢献したいと願っております。
どうぞよろしくお願いいたします。
```

(Translation: "I am majoring in computer science, studying Python and data structures & algorithms. I am strongly attracted to your company's ___ business, and want to work in your technical and global environment. I have passed JLPT N5 and want to continue improving my Japanese while contributing to your ___ business. Thank you for your consideration.")

**Fill in the blanks:**
- 事業内容 (business): e.g. ECプラットフォーム (e-commerce platform), 決済サービス (payment services), モバイルアプリ開発 (mobile app development)
- 御社の (company name): use 御社 (your company) — never write the company name directly in 志望動機

## Rules
- Handwriting: black ink only. If typed, use a standard Japanese resume template (A4)
- Dates: 和暦 or 西暦 — be consistent (use 西暦 = western calendar, safer for foreigners)
- Do NOT leave blank lines in the middle of sections
- Use a proper Rirekisho template (free PDF templates: "履歴書テンプレート" from マイナビ or リクナビ, or buy from 100-yen store / download from キャリアチケット)
- If you cannot write Japanese yet: fill the factual parts in Japanese (dates, institution names), write 志望動機 in Japanese with the template above, and note your JLPT level honestly
