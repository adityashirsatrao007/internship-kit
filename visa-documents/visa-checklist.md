# Visa Documents — Paid Internship in Japan (Designated Activities No. 9)

**Researched from:** Immigration Services Agency of Japan (moj.go.jp), visaoffice.jp, HR visa guides, lawoffice-yokoyama.com.

## First: which visa do you need?

| Your situation | Visa status | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Overseas university student, PAID internship in Japan | **Designated Activities (No. 9)** | The main one for paid internships. Host company applies for Certificate of Eligibility (CoE) |
| Overseas student, UNPAID internship ≤ 90 days | **Temporary Visitor** (visa waiver) | No CoE needed; visa waiver if your country qualifies |
| Overseas student, UNPAID internship > 90 days | **Cultural Activities** | Must prove self-funding |
| Already in Japan on Student visa | Work permission (資格外活動許可) | Standard cap 28 hrs/week; over 28 hrs needs individual permission |

**You are in the paid-internship case → Designated Activities (No. 9).**

## Designated Activities (No. 9) eligibility — ALL must be true
1. Enrolled in an overseas university in a **degree-granting program** (online/correspondence NOT eligible)
2. Age 18+ at entry
3. Internship is **part of your academic curriculum** (for credit)
4. Formal **agreement between your university + the Japanese host company** (tripartite)
5. Work relates to your **academic major**
6. Duration ≤ 1 year, and ≤ **half** your degree program
7. Paid interns are treated as **workers** — minimum wage applies

## Who does what (division of labor)
- **Japanese host company** applies for the **Certificate of Eligibility (CoE)** at the regional immigration bureau (this is the company's job, not yours) — takes ~1–3 months
- **You** then apply for the visa at the Japanese embassy/consulate in your country (issued in ~1–2 weeks once you have the CoE)
- **Your home university** must sign the tripartite agreement and confirm the internship is credit-bearing

## Documents YOU need to prepare

| # | Document | Notes |

## Timeline (plan backwards)
- Offer signed: T-4 months
- Company files CoE: T-3 months (CoE takes 1–3 months)
- CoE received: T-1 month
- Embassy visa application: T-1 to T-2 weeks
- Arrive in Japan: on time

**⚠️ Start talking to your university's international office NOW.** The tripartite agreement is the #1 blocker — many universities are slow or unfamiliar with this. Confirm early that your degree program allows credit-bearing overseas internships.

## Important legal notes
- Designated Activities (No. 9) is **not renewable and not convertible** to a work visa directly. If the internship leads to a full-time job, you must change status to the Engineer visa (技術・人文知識・国際業務) which requires a bachelor's degree (or 10 years experience).
- You may NOT do work beyond the approved internship scope.
- Programs that handle the visa for you: **Mercari** (visa support), **Woven by Toyota**, **HENNGE**, **METI JIP** (government, lowest friction), **Sony**.

## If you're already in Japan (Student visa)
- Need 資格外活動許可 (permission for activities outside status)
- Standard: 28 hrs/week during term
- For internships over 28 hrs/week: individual permission, generally only for final-year undergrad / 2nd-year grad students with 90%+ credits earned

## METI Japan Internship Program (JIP) — easiest government path
- Japanese government program matching foreign students/graduates to Japanese SMEs
- Handles visa, flights, accommodation; allowance up to ¥90,000/month + ¥3,000/day
- Requires JLPT N3/CEFR B1 OR English; age 18–39; not currently residing in Japan
- Apply via internshipprogram.go.jp (deadlines ~June each year)
- ⚠️ The internship is training, not employment — no guarantee of a job after
