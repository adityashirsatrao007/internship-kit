# Cover Letter — Goldman Sachs / JPMorgan (Banks)

**IMPORTANT (researched from eFinancialCareers, livecareer, Finbound, Leland):**
- **Goldman Sachs recruiters LOVE cover letters.** Unlike tech companies, banking still reads them.
- Goldman format: **~300 words** (some say 200–350, aim for ~300). Explains: interest in role + firm + suitability, and cites specific points from your CV.
- No formal cover letter field in most GS Summer Analyst apps (community-reported) — but if asked, or if you want to include, use this.
- Banks care about: division fit, quantitative results, the "why this team" story, accuracy.
- JPMorgan: applications often use word-limited questions in the portal instead of a free letter. Adapt this content to answer those.

I am drawn to Goldman Sachs because of how it combines [technology at scale / data-driven engineering] with the rigor of a global financial institution — and specifically to [Engineering division] because [specific reason, e.g., the platform engineering / the way GS runs its tech org].

[Paragraph 2 — proof with numbers. Banks like quant.]
In my [Project Name], I [built / analyzed] [X] using [tech], achieving [quantified result: reduced X by X% / processed X records / handled X requests]. I have completed [N] projects end-to-end with [metric]. I bring [skill relevant to engineering division, e.g., Python for data analysis, strong systems thinking, clean testing discipline]. [Optional: name-drop — e.g., "I spoke with [Name] at [event]" — only if true.]

[Paragraph 3 — why banking / why them.]
I want to build software where correctness and scale matter under real financial constraints, and I believe [Engineering division] is the best place to learn that discipline. [One honest reason tied to their work.]

[Close — call to action.]
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my background further. Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

## Goldman-specific rules
- ~300 words max, 1 page A4, Calibri/Arial 10–12pt, 1-inch margins
- Video interview (30 min) + HackerRank assessment follow the resume screen — so your letter must match what's on the resume (one consistent story)
- Rolling review: apply in the first 1–2 weeks of the window, but polished, not rushed
- 4 division/location combinations max per recruiting year — choose wisely
- Personal statement field (if offered, 300 words or less): use it to connect past work to the division — NOT a generic cover letter
