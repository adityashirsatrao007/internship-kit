# Cover Letter — Japanese Companies (Mercari / Rakuten / Sony / LY Corp)

**Format rules:**
- 1 page, Arial/Calibri 10–12pt, 1-inch margins, PDF
- 250–400 words
- Japanese English-first companies read English letters. Do NOT write this in Japanese unless the posting is in Japanese.
- Mercari: cover letter optional but recommended; the app form has long-answer questions instead.
